# Separate composited motion study

This branch is a clearly labeled alternative study. It does not replace PR #5 or production. Start with /solar-study on this branch's Netlify Deploy Preview.

The original generated launch and Earth-departure files are unchanged. A locally rendered, 24-second canvas journey adds the Moon, Mars, a distant Sun, and a slow arrival at the original galaxy artwork. This is composited motion, not newly generated continuous film, and it is not an astronomical scale model. The hero is full background on desktop and phone; portrait cover framing crops the original wide image.

The requested headline is unchanged: “Make your go-to-market move at the speed of your product.” All business copy outside the study label is unchanged. Pause, Replay, Skip, Still image, offscreen/hidden pause and reduced-motion/Save-Data/no-JavaScript/deep-link/error fallbacks remain.

NASA imagery sources are in assets/video/solar/SOURCES.md and linked visibly from the preview banner. This study uses the existing generated launch footage and locally rendered motion; it does not claim a new continuous generated film.

Tests: normal build/copy/references, five contact tests, baseline browser and responsive sweep, cinematic flow/fallback tests, and focused real playback through each new phase at 390/1440. The focused script checks the absence of copy panels, scene-darkening overlays and media masks; white glyph edges/shadows; pause freezing; and a stationary headline. Background-only contrast is reported without a pass claim because it excludes the glyph outline/shadow used for legibility. Real launch and galaxy frames are also visually reviewed. This is not a complete WCAG contrast certification. Current receipts and screenshots are in preview-review/solar-bridge. Baseline mobile Lighthouse scored 97 with CLS 0; that baseline score is not a performance claim for this canvas study. Physical iOS/Safari is untested.

No merge, production promotion, or product acceptance is established by this visual study.
