Person by person: what Coordinated sent in seven weeks

Every card is logged against the person who received it, with the reason it was sent. Counts are measured on the fixture (33 cards, 9 notes, seven weeks, nine people); reading minutes are estimates at the reading speed set above. The pilot readout uses this ledger format; its figures come from that pilot.

Thirty-three cards needed an answer, nine settlement notes needed reading, and every person read twelve channel posts, seven of them Monday pictures. The sponsor and the people who own nothing on the launch answered nothing. The week a person moves their own date is the week they hear from us: Maya moved hers, and five of her ten cards came that week. The Thursday brief derived "No meeting needed: nothing is waiting on a person in the room."

The question we ask at formation

Which recurring meeting does this launch retire, and from when?

The hours above come from that answer, and from nothing else. If every meeting stays, Coordinated adds work. The sponsor answers it on day one, the default being that the Monday picture replaces the status meeting from week three once it has been right for two, and the readout says whether the meeting stayed retired.
