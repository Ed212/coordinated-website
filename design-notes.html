Implementation notes

Direction, contribution,
and the decision between them.

The homepage introduces the living-plan value through one fictional launch. The example lets a visitor trace an explicit dependency, contribute a concern, and distinguish an answer from an authorized amendment.

Visual system

The existing monochrome galaxy anchors the hero. Subsequent visuals show the work itself: a connected goal and contribution, changed dates, a recorded response, and an unresolved review question. Product illustrations are readable HTML, not generated screenshots.

State and boundaries

The walkthrough runs locally without persistence. The email form only prepares a draft. Static use-case cards are labeled illustrations. Customer evidence, fixed commercial terms, and security claims have not been fabricated.

Human participation

Sarah can propose a date, ask to talk, or remain unanswered. Only an explicit example decision by Nadia amends the plan. Every route keeps missing legal approval visible. A changed response invalidates the old decision.

Motion and access

The page has no automatic narrative progression or galaxy motion. Navigation, disclosure elements, and controls are keyboard accessible. Reduced-motion preferences disable smooth scrolling. The example has a readable no-JavaScript fallback.
