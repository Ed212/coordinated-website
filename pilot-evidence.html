Pilot measures · Methodology

Does the plan get clearer
as coordination gets lighter?

That is what a pilot should establish. We define the initiative, baseline, and review window together before measuring a change. Customer results are not yet published.

01

Chase load per week

Count direct follow-ups needed to obtain an owner, response, condition, or decision for the selected initiative. Record operator and participant effort, including correction and setup work, so the burden cannot simply move out of view.

Compare baseline and pilot windows

02

Time from reported change to decision

Measure from the first shared report to the authorized decision. Report unresolved cases and their elapsed time separately alongside the median for completed decisions.

Include open cases and sample size

03

Decisions before the affected deadline

Count decisions recorded before the earliest downstream lock or deadline, out of every decision due in the agreed window. Missed deadlines and still-open decisions stay in the denominator.

Report decided on time / all decisions due

04

Responses still pending at the deadline

Count requested answers still missing when the affected deadline arrives. People only informed are excluded. An unanswered request never counts as agreement.

Report pending requests and affected decisions

05

Review preparation time

Record operator time spent reconstructing the initiative state before the review. Track draft corrections and participant interruptions as well as preparation, so apparent savings do not hide extra work.

Compare time and quality of the review brief

The readout

Publish what happened.
Keep the limits visible.

Record the baseline, pilot duration, number of changes, people involved, and any missing observations. Separate observed outcomes from estimates and explain differences in workload between the comparison windows.

Ask participants whether their work was represented accurately and whether they could see how a concern was addressed. Faster answers alone are not proof of better coordination.

Customer names, quotes, and results appear only after observation and permission. A pilot is evidence about a particular team and workflow, not a guaranteed result for every company.
