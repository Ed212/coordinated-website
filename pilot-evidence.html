Evidence / Definitions before results

Research and manual exploration

Check decisions, delivery and the result.

Coordinated is prelaunch, with no launched users. The software and product pilot are not ready. Any manual exploration needs separately agreed scope, permissions and verified delivery readiness. It would start from an outcome or analysis and early stakeholder consultation, with documents optional. Work done by hand in Slack is not evidence of autonomous software.

Define one initiative, owners, baseline and inspection windows before a test. Distinguish a manual exploration, prototype/integration test, independent product evaluation and real customer outcomes. The simulator comparison on the homepage establishes none of those customer results.

Six proposed evidence areas

01

Total human coordination effort

Count operator, stakeholder and our manual-service minutes: onboarding, domain answers, decisions, interruptions, verification, corrections and follow-through. Record human rescue separately, including manually assembling dependencies, relaying answers or chasing owners. Hidden rescue invalidates autonomy claims.

02

Decision and delivery timing

Check launch-plan completeness, missing owners, approvals, gates, dependencies, parallel work and required sequencing. Check readiness evidence with accountable owners, and whether constraints were discovered, and decisions reached the right authority before the last useful action time derived from confirmed lead times. Compare accepted delivery dates with actual delivery evidence, separately from decision latency. Keep unanswered decisions, delays and unnecessary interruptions visible.

03

Accepted changes and observed effects

Keep scoped decision approval, specialist gates, exact feasible delivery acceptance and action permission separate. Check whether accepted changes reached affected work and verify the actual authorized effects. Count unsupported commitments, duplicate or unauthorized actions and missed effects. A message receipt is not agreement or completed work.

04

Delivery and later business results

Verify delivered scope separately from business results. Compare actual evidence with the pinned original and current approved baseline and target. Each measure needs a definition, unit, source, human evidence owner, cohort, review window and freshness. Report missing or inconclusive evidence and the human decision/next inspection; release alone closes no benefit check.

05

Work actually removed

Record which setup, chasing or review work people stopped doing and which remained. Count meetings actually retired only when participants confirm it. Compare like-for-like facts and permissions; extra setup and correction effort can consume any savings.

06

Assumptions and the next decision

Check each due assumption separately from a missed outcome metric. Name the inspection owner, date, evidence source and whether it supports, contradicts or leaves the assumption unresolved. The authorized human makes the disposition; missing or stale evidence cannot close the check. Record an accepted change, an explicit no-change or defer decision, and the next owner and inspection.

Method

The definition does not move after the result arrives.

The operator and relevant owners agree the initiative, authority, sources, definitions and windows before the test. Preserve original and explicitly amended goals, approved context and exact plan versions. Report observations with their limitations and all human effort; a before/after change does not by itself establish causality.

No invented benchmark, extrapolated saving or unnamed customer quote appears in the readout.
