Illustrative scenarios / Northgate · Atlas launch

Six moments when the record has to move before the work does.

Memory can recover what people said. These cards show what each person was asked to accept, the source sentence behind it, and what stayed on record after the answer.

Northgate, Atlas, and the people below are fictional. These are independent examples, not a single chronological customer story. The cards are static illustrations; try the complete interactive example.
