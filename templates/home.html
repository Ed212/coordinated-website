Without Coordinated

Your launch lead holds the threads together.

  1. Reconstruct the impact. The launch lead hears about the delay, searches documents and messages, and works out who is affected.
  2. Chase the answers. Ask Product, Pricing, Engineering, Marketing, Sales, Support and Security what changes for their work and dates.
  3. Reconcile the launch. Bring the options to the decision makers, confirm each owner’s work and update the plan across teams.

The planned Coordinated workflow

The launch lead has a program manager to help.

  1. Find the affected work. Use the current launch plan and approved scope, feature, pricing and security information to identify dependencies.
  2. Ask the right owners. Check what each owner can deliver and by when, collect readiness evidence and keep missing answers visible.
  3. Bring one decision forward. Put the options in front of Product, then coordinate the revised work as people approve changes and owners confirm it.

A decision, then owner confirmation

Product approves a core-only launch. Pricing approves the revised claims. Engineering provides core-readiness evidence; Marketing, Sales and Support confirm their updated work and dates. The release owner checks the required gates before giving the core launch the go-ahead. Export still waits for Security clearance.

How it works

Manage the work of the launch.

This is the Slack workflow we’re designing, shown through Northstar’s launch. The same plan connects the change, the decision and each owner’s confirmation.

  1. Start with the current plan

    Start with the launch goal, approved scope and responsible owners. Here, export’s security review changes the plan.

  2. Map the affected work

    Check packaging, pricing, the demo, launch content and support. Core drafts can continue; changed claims and export must wait.

  3. Check with owners

    Ask what each owner can deliver and by when. Check evidence for readiness and keep unanswered questions visible.

  4. Put the decision in front of people

    Product approves the core-only scope. Pricing approves the changed commercial claims. Required Security gates remain in place.

  5. Confirm the revised launch

    Owners confirm their revised work and dates. The release owner checks the core gates and makes the go/no-go decision; export stays held.
