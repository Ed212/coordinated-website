01 / How it works

One change propagates through one record.

01 / 03

Hand it what you already wrote.

Coordinated proposes the initiative, people, commitments and dependencies, each with the sentence it came from. The operator reviews one draft.

02 / 03

Owners accept, condition, or decline.

Nothing is a commitment until a person answers. Conditions and proposed dates stay attached to the terms they qualify.

03 / 03

The review opens settled.

When a date moves, the people it reaches are named before it moves. Cards update in place, answers return, and the terms move to v2.

01 · A date slips

“Staging slipped to Saturday. Who needs to know before Sarah's spend locks Tuesday at 5?”

Sarah is asked: her announcement needs ten calendar days after staging and Sat Oct 10 leaves five. Marco is informed: his Acme condition still holds by one business day. Nadia decides; the forecast is on record either way.

  • Launch plan ¶7 · ten days after staging
  • Delivery notes, line 11 · Acme rollout Thu Oct 15
  • Spend lock · Tue Oct 6, 5:00 PM

Decided Tue Oct 6, 9:05 AM · 1 asked, answered · 1 informed · 0 pending · terms v2

02 · A scope changes

“Nadia removed self-serve import on Wed Oct 7. Which promises now need an answer?”

Sarah is asked because the launch announcement names the import. Marco is asked because the Acme rollout includes it. Dana is informed because the support guide no longer needs the import path.

  • Launch plan ¶4 · self-serve import in scope
  • Acme notes, line 8 · import workflow Thu Oct 15
  • Support brief ¶3 · guide due Mon Oct 12

Decided Thu Oct 8, 11:20 AM · 2 asked, answered · 1 informed · 0 pending · scope v2

03 · An owner leaves

“Maya is out through Fri Oct 16. Who owns the staging decision due Thu Oct 8?”

Nadia is asked to name temporary authority. Josh is informed because the launch cannot move without that decision. No replacement is inferred; ownership stays unseen until a person accepts it.

  • Review notes ¶6 · Maya owns staging readiness
  • Atlas terms v2 · decision due Thu Oct 8, 2:00 PM
  • Team update · Maya unavailable through Fri Oct 16

Recorded Wed Oct 7, 4:40 PM · Josh accepted interim authority · 0 pending · ownership v2

04 · A promise is made to a customer

“Marco promised Acme a Thu Oct 15 rollout. Does the current plan support it?”

Marco is asked to confirm the condition. Nadia decides whether it becomes part of the Atlas terms. Sarah is informed because her announcement uses the same rollout date.

  • Acme thread · rollout Thu Oct 15
  • Launch plan ¶7 · staging Sat Oct 10
  • Announcement brief · publication Thu Oct 15, 8:00 AM

Accepted Mon Oct 5, 2:10 PM · customer term recorded · Marco owns the update · terms v2

05 · An assumption comes due

“The rollout assumes legal approval by Mon Oct 12. What happens if it is not in?”

Nadia is asked to decide before the approval window closes. Sarah and Marco are informed because their Thu Oct 15 work depends on the answer. The record labels legal approval unseen until someone posts it.

  • Atlas assumptions ¶2 · legal approval Mon Oct 12
  • Campaign brief · spend starts Tue Oct 13, 9:00 AM
  • Acme notes · rollout Thu Oct 15

Answered Fri Oct 9, 3:30 PM · condition added · review Mon Oct 12, 9:00 AM · 0 pending

06 · A review starts

“What is already settled before the Tue Oct 13 review?”

The staging forecast is decided, the import is out of scope, Marco's customer condition is recorded, and every affected owner has answered. The brief opens with the remaining decision, not a reconstruction.

  • Atlas terms v2 · accepted Thu Oct 8, 11:20 AM
  • Owner responses · through Mon Oct 12, 4:15 PM
  • Review brief · generated Tue Oct 13, 8:00 AM

Review opened Tue Oct 13, 9:00 AM · 3 changes settled · 0 decisions reconstructed

05 / Trust

The boundary is visible.

What it reads

Documents you hand it, artifacts posted in claimed channels, and threads where it is tagged.

What it never reads

Channels on its own, direct messages, or meetings. It labels what it cannot see.

What a channel sees

Counts and state, never restricted terms. Each person receives only the card that belongs to them.

Architecture

A record people and agents read the same.

Only people can change what has been agreed. Agents read the accepted decisions, guardrails and plan before they act.
