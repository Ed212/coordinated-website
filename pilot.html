Before you begin

A few practical answers.

Do I need to send a document or install anything now?

No. Start with a short description. If the pilot is a fit, we agree the source material and Slack setup together before connecting anything.

How long does a pilot take, and what does it cost?

We scope the pilot around one initiative and an upcoming review. Duration, participant effort, pricing, and what happens afterward are agreed with you before starting.

What happens to documents shared during a pilot?

Before sharing, ask us to confirm who can access them, how long they are retained, how deletion works, which AI providers process them, and whether any provider may use them for model training. We will agree the applicable arrangements before connecting your sources.

Read the current privacy policy →
Does everyone have to move their work into Coordinated?

No company-wide rollout is needed to start. We focus on a selected initiative and its important agreements. Tasks stay in your existing tools; the pilot uses Slack for the coordination around them.

Are the Atlas examples customer results?

No. Atlas and Northgate are fictional examples. We do not yet publish customer outcomes. A pilot measures the actual coordination work, including missing answers and unresolved decisions.
