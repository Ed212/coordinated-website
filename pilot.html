Before you begin

No mysteries
at the starting line.

What does it cost?

Pilot pricing is scoped with you. We agree a written price, what is included, and any continuation terms before starting. The first fit conversation carries no purchase commitment.

What is ready, and what is still being developed?

The offer is a founder-supported design-partner pilot around a shared agreement, targeted responses, change handling, and a review brief. We confirm the exact workflow with you. The website is illustrative; broader company coverage, agent integrations, and live connectors are not automatically included.

What if we use Jira, Linear, or spreadsheets?

Keep them. The pilot begins with the relevant documents or updates you choose to share. We agree any connected tools separately, so a connector is not a hidden prerequisite for starting.

Does everyone need to adopt another workspace?

The proposed workflow puts coordination cards and review material in Slack. The operator maintains the plan with our help; other participants respond only where their input is needed. We measure the effort on both sides.

What do our agents get?

The direction is a scoped, versioned statement of the current goal, approved guidance, constraints, and unresolved questions. If you bring a compatible agent workflow, we can scope a separate integration experiment. Reading an update is not proof of compliance; enforcement requires a gate at the action boundary.

Who can see our information?

We agree the audience and source access before connecting an initiative. We also confirm retention, deletion, AI providers, and processing terms with you. Start with a non-confidential description; don’t send internal documents through this form.

What if there is no meaningful change during the pilot?

We can rehearse a real prior change to test the workflow. We label that as a simulation and do not claim reduced live coordination effort or earlier intervention from it. We decide together whether an extension would produce useful evidence.

What happens when the pilot ends?

Review the record and the measures together. Continue only on agreed terms, adjust if there is a specific fix worth testing, or stop. Export format, access after the pilot, and data deletion are included in the agreement made before kickoff.
