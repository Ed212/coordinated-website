# Preview review corrections

The November 4 preview responds to the supplied independent review summary and the actual site behavior. It keeps the product-speed headline, category, illustrative seven-team scenario and existing brand.

- The audience is now explicit in the hero. The concrete Northstar card is alongside the headline on desktop and immediately follows the copy on mobile; the galaxy is secondary.
- The hero’s former pressed-state-only controls are real links. “See affected work” opens and scrolls to the seven-team dependency map. “Review open decisions” focuses the next decision and accountable owners. Neither approves or sends anything.
- The primary navigation uses consistent Example / How it works / Evidence / Contact destinations. Section labels no longer skip numbered chapters. Contact CTAs consistently use the established mailto address.
- The public submission form is removed. Opening an email link is explicitly not a send or delivery confirmation. The retained legacy handler confirms configured mail-service recipient acceptance only, and disclaims inbox delivery; unconfirmed acceptance does not invite an uninformed retry.
- Privacy copy now covers the marketing website and contact enquiries. Unsupported encryption, general access-control, regular assessment, live integration/account, retention and administrator-control assertions were removed. Homepage permission and authority boundaries are labeled as proposed design rather than verified production controls.
- The reported font cause was not established. The headline now keeps “go-to-market” together and uses less aggressive tracking. Responsive checks explicitly test overflow and hero collisions rather than assuming the reported defect.

Validation: build/copy/reference checks, five injected contact tests, eight browser cases (400/1100px; both themes and generated variants), no-JavaScript email access, and a six-width 320–1920px collision/overflow sweep pass. Browser checks submit no forms. Local minimum hero contrast is 7.64:1 mobile and 7.99:1 desktop. Mobile Lighthouse is 97 for both variants, CLS 0. Fresh screenshots/logs are retained in the task workspace; no real contact message was sent.
