01 / How it works

One change propagates through one record.

01 / 03

Hand it what you already wrote.

Coordinated proposes the initiative, people, commitments and dependencies, each with the sentence it came from. The operator reviews one draft.

02 / 03

Nothing is a commitment until a person answers. Conditions and proposed dates stay attached to the terms they qualify.

The review opens settled.

When a date moves, the people it reaches are named before it moves. Cards update in place, answers return, and the terms move to v2.

05 / Trust

The boundary is visible.

What it reads

Documents you hand it, artifacts posted in claimed channels, and threads where it is tagged.

What it never reads

Channels on its own, direct messages, or meetings. It labels what it cannot see.

What a channel sees

Counts and state, never restricted terms. Each person receives only the card that belongs to them.

What we are building

Three agents, one record.

The design gives the driver the work of preparing decisions, the verifier the work of checking claims, and the aligner the work of comparing accepted terms with the sponsor's intended outcome. These agents are planned architecture, not available pilot automation.

Accepted, supported, or unknown.

We are building a verifier that will mark every claim as one of three things: accepted by a named person, supported by current evidence with the period it covers, or unknown. Nothing on the page or in a message is presented as true because someone accepted it.

Guardrails as code.

We plan deterministic checks over each proposed message and change. The checks will return a refusal when a rule is not met, with a person resolving what the record cannot settle.

A read contract for agents.

We are building a read contract so agents will be able to ask who decides, what was agreed and what changed. Only people will be able to change the accepted terms.

The record holds pointers to your documents, with the version and the person who decides each, and keeps the evidence you hand it under your retention policy.
