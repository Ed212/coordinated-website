03 / Clear direction. Meaningful participation.

Know why it matters.
Know where you stand.

For operators

Less to chase.
More room to decide.

See what changed, who needs to answer, and what remains unresolved before the next review. Spend less time reconstructing the plan and carrying updates between teams.

For contributors

Your work has context.
Your input stays attached.

Understand what your work contributes to and who depends on it. Raise a concern or propose a condition, then see how it was addressed in the decision.

For leaders

See the trade-off.
Keep the reasoning.

See the commitments and constraints behind the plan, alongside the choices that need your judgment. Carry the reason for a change to the people doing the work.
