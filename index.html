03 / A plan that moves with reality

The change finds its people.
The decision finds its way back.

Self-adjusting means the context, impact, and questions move forward automatically. Changes to commitments still have an accountable decision-maker.

  1. 01

    Notice the change.

    A selected meeting note, shared update, or connected source brings new information into the plan.

    Forecast ≠ agreement
  2. 02

    Ask with context.

    The affected owner sees what changed, why it matters, what they rely on, and when an answer is needed.

    Silence ≠ consent
  3. 03

    Resolve the trade-off.

    The operator gets the options, consequences, and people’s input. The authorized owner makes the call.

    Input stays with the decision
  4. 04

    Carry it forward.

    The agreed plan, affected people’s context, and next-review brief carry the decision and its reasoning.

    Evidence closes the loop

Before we talk

A little more clarity.

An honest starting point makes a better pilot.

Is this a new project management tool?

Keep tasks and delivery detail where your team already works. Coordinated connects the important cross-functional agreements to company goals, assumptions, decisions, and your next review.

Will everyone have another system to maintain?

The pilot is operator-led. Participants answer only where their commitment or input is needed, through the agreed Slack workflow. We measure participant effort as well as the operator’s time so work does not simply move elsewhere.

What does “self-adjusting” actually mean?

New information can update forecasts, surface affected commitments, and prepare questions or review material. It does not silently rewrite commitments, infer consent, or choose trade-offs for your company. Those changes need the designated authority.

Is this available to try today?

We are inviting design partners to a scoped, founder-supported pilot. The website examples are interactive illustrations, not a live connection to your company. Agent integrations and live source connectors are optional and must be confirmed in scope.

What if our information is sensitive?

Start with a non-confidential description. Before connecting sources, agree audience, access, retention, deletion, and AI processing terms. A pilot does not require opening every channel.
