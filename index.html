Why

The launch moved on a Tuesday. By Thursday, sales was quoting the old date, marketing had a page queued for a feature that no longer existed, and nobody had lied or forgotten. The plan changed and nothing told the people executing the old version.

Tools are good at reminding. None of them do invalidation, because none of them recorded what each person actually agreed to.

Then the executors started to include agents, and an agent will execute a superseded plan perfectly. It can't overhear that the launch moved.

How it works

The plan is a record.

Every commitment has an owner, a date, a scope, and the people who depend on it.

A change goes to the people it affects.

Not a broadcast. Each person gets their own delta and confirms, proposes a date, or says they're blocked.
