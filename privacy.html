Privacy Policy

Last updated: Wed Nov 4, 2026

Coordinated is prelaunch. This policy covers this marketing website and enquiries you choose to send us. Data handling for a future product or pilot would need separately documented terms.

Information You Choose to Share

Email links open your email app. If you send an enquiry, we process the contact details and message content you provide so we can respond. Messages delivered through our email service are also processed by that provider. This website has no account signup, workplace connection or document-upload flow.

Please do not send documents, credentials or private launch data through this website or an initial enquiry.

Hosting and Network Information

Loading this website sends network and technical information to the providers serving its pages and assets. Some hosted previews also include the hosting provider’s collaboration tools. These providers’ own terms and policies apply to their services.

How We Use Enquiries

We use information you email us to respond and discuss potential research or a future pilot. Our email provider processes messages as part of delivering that service.

Security and Future Pilots

Any future pilot would need separately agreed scope, permissions and data-handling terms, with security measures documented for that scope before private information is shared.

Questions and Requests

You can contact us about information in an enquiry, including a request to review, correct or delete it. We will review the request with you; this page does not promise a fixed retention period or an automated deletion process.

Contact ed@coordinatedai.ai.

Changes to This Page

We may update this page as the website and proposed product develop. The date above identifies this version.
